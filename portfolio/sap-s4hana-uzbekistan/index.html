SAP S/4HANA implementation at three thermal power plants in Uzbekistan

About client

Our client is a company that manages energy assets in Uzbekistan: power generation and supply, modernisation and development of the country's energy infrastructure.

The project covered three thermal power plants with a combined installed capacity of approximately 5,000 MW, located in the capital region, one of the country's central regions, and an autonomous republic in the west. The two largest plants have been in operation since the early 1960s and remain important sources of electricity and heat for the capital and central regions.

Overview

Best-Run Solutions implemented an ERP system based on SAP S/4HANA at three thermal power plants in Uzbekistan, located in the capital region, one of the country's central regions, and an autonomous republic in the west. Our team worked on site at each facility.

Three power plants, three separate contracts, one solution template, and one project team. The contractual scope was the same for every site, but in practice each plant had its own requirements: accounting specifics, data quality and availability, and equipment operating practices. We adapted the single template to the needs of each facility and delivered a separate set of project documentation for each of them.

At every plant the solution covers four functional areas (SAP FI, MM, PM, SD): finance, including financial, tax and management accounting, fixed assets and IFRS; procurement and inventory management; plant maintenance and repair; and sales management. The target model includes 44 business processes in 11 groups for each plant, detailed down to 149 operations and fully mapped to the technical specification. Customisation was kept to a minimum: most requirements were covered by standard SAP S/4HANA functionality.

Each power plant is an independent joint-stock company with its own steering committee. Every project milestone was therefore reviewed three times, with three meetings, three protocols and three sets of decisions, while the solution and the team remained shared.

The project team included 32 Best-Run Solutions consultants in 6 groups, with up to 26 specialists working simultaneously at peak. The team covered procurement and logistics, plant maintenance, finance and accounting, integration and data migration, development and Basis, solution architecture and project management.

Project duration: 12 months, 5 stages: project preparation, business blueprint, realisation, final preparation and pilot operation.

Project tasks

  • One SAP S/4HANA template for three power plants
  • Finance and IFRS, procurement, maintenance and sales on a single platform
  • Data migration and training of 380 end users
  • Pilot operation and go-live preparation at each plant

Project results

The project was delivered on schedule with no delays. All five stages have been accepted by the steering committees of the three power plants, and the system is ready for production use.
