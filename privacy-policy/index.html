Privacy Policy — Best-Run Solutions Sp. z o.o.

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · Version 2.0 (replaces the version of 29 July 2025)

This Privacy Policy explains how Best-Run Solutions Sp. z o.o. processes the personal data of visitors to bestrunsolutions.com and of people who contact us through the website, by e-mail or via messengers, apply for our vacancies, or represent our clients, prospective clients, partners and suppliers. It is issued under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), the Polish Act of 10 May 2018 on the protection of personal data and Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej) on cookies.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer because we are not required to do so under Article 37 GDPR. If we appoint one, we will publish the contact details here.

This Policy covers the website bestrunsolutions.com, the contact form, correspondence with us by e-mail and messengers, recruitment, and our LinkedIn company page to the extent we act as a controller. It does not cover personal data we process on behalf of clients as a processor under a separate data processing agreement, for example in SAP implementation or support projects.

The website is intended for business users. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data of persons under 16.

Is providing data mandatory?

Providing personal data is voluntary; no law requires you to provide it. However:

  • without your name and e-mail we cannot answer your inquiry; other fields of the form are optional;

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR). Advertising tools may add your browser to audience lists for showing our ads only with your consent.

3. Linking your inquiry to the advertising source

When you send the contact form, we store together with your inquiry information on how you reached our website:

  • the Google Ads click identifier (GCLID), if you came from our ad;
  • UTM parameters of the link you used (source, medium, campaign, keyword, ad content);
  • the first page you opened on the website and the page from which you sent the form.

We store this information only if you have consented to advertising cookies. Without your consent, the form is sent without these fields.

We use it to understand which campaigns and keywords bring real business inquiries and to improve our advertising.

We may also report back to Google Ads which clicks led to an inquiry and whether the inquiry became a qualified lead or a contract (offline conversion import). For this we send Google only the GCLID, the type of event and its date and time. We never send Google your name, phone number, e-mail address or the content of your message.

For conversion measurement Google acts as our processor under the Google Ads Data Processing Terms. These data are kept together with your inquiry for the period stated in section 7.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy (Art. 15);
  • rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format, where processing is based on consent or contract and is automated (Art. 20);
  • object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21); if you object to direct marketing, we stop it without further assessment;
  • withdraw any consent at any time — by e-mail or, for cookies, via "Cookie settings"; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

To exercise your rights, write to info@bestrunsolutions.com. We will reply within one month; for complex requests this period may be extended by two further months, and we will tell you why. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

9. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (Art. 32 GDPR): encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access limited to staff who need it for their work, up-to-date software and spam protection on forms. Inquiries are delivered to a company mailbox accessible only to authorised staff.

10. Changes to this Policy

We update this Policy when our processing or the law changes. The current version and its effective date are always published at https://bestrunsolutions.com/privacy-policy/; we also announce material changes on the website.
