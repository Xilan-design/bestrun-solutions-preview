Individual Specialists or an Agreed Project Team.

We can discuss an individual specialist or a dedicated team with an agreed composition. Team management depends on the project and is most often handled by Best-Run Solutions. Teams can be scaled during an engagement by agreement. If a specialist isn’t the right fit, we’ll arrange a replacement under agreed terms.

Best-Run Solutions assesses specialist competencies through a technical interview before presenting a candidate. Customers can also conduct their own technical interview. If no matching specialist is on the bench, sourcing typically takes 3–5 business days, followed by additional interview and documentation time. This is an indicative process, not a guaranteed start date.

Engagement Conditions Agreed for the Project.

Minimum engagement is three months. Specialists can work remotely or work on-site when needed. Specific start dates, availability, replacement conditions and team size are agreed individually.
