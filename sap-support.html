SAP Support with Clear SLAs and L1–L3 Escalation.

Best-Run Solutions provides SAP application and system support, incident processing, administration and operational maintenance within agreed service arrangements. Support hours, priorities and SLA targets are defined by contract.

100+ SAP support projects

Support Built Around Real Operational Needs.

Typical support challenges include insufficient internal capacity, untracked requests, repeated incidents without documented solutions and limited visibility into request handling. We establish a support process around the agreed landscape, responsibilities and ticket workflow.

Technical Support Across SAP Operations.

SAP application and system administration

Administration of SAP application operations, system settings and supported SAP environments.

Monitoring and recovery

Monitoring SAP systems and restoring service quickly after failures.

Updates and technical maintenance

Installation of contracted updates, including delta upgrades where applicable, and configuration of print services.

Performance and database troubleshooting

Analysis and resolution of SAP performance and database issues within the support scope.

Integrations and interfaces

Investigation and resolution of issues affecting integrations between SAP and third-party systems.

User access and roles

User administration, role configuration and access-permission settings.

Incident and problem resolution

Registration, investigation and troubleshooting of support incidents and requests within the agreed scope.

Application changes and enhancements

Configuration changes, ABAP development and functionality enhancements when included in the support contract.

Documentation and knowledge transfer

Documentation of changes, user instructions and maintenance of knowledge on resolved issues.

User and administrator guidance

Consultations, support and training for SAP users and administrators as agreed.

The exact modules, responsibilities, change activities and covered systems are defined for the support agreement.

SAP licence-related and hardware infrastructure support are also named in the corporate support presentation; their applicability should be reviewed for the requested contract scope.

A Traceable SAP Support Request Process.

  1. 01

    Register the request

    Support request registration and management in SAP Solution Manager.

  2. 02

    Clarify, classify and prioritise

    Clarify the issue, classify the request and establish its priority.

  3. 03

    Assign responsibility

    Assign responsibility within the agreed support model.

  4. 04

    Investigate and resolve

    Troubleshooting and resolution within the agreed support scope.

  5. 05

    Escalate if needed

    Escalation to the next support level or vendor where relevant.

  6. 06

    Notify, document and analyse

    Status updates, documentation and analysis of the request.

SAP Solution Manager is used for registering and tracking requests. Process functions include assignment, priority handling, deadline control, status updates, knowledge-base development and reporting within the contracted framework.

Three Support Levels, with Vendor Escalation When Required.

L1

Request intake, classification, initial troubleshooting, straightforward cases and routing.

L2

Module-focused consulting, analysis and incident resolution; specifications for changes where needed.

L3

Expert investigation, configuration changes, ABAP developments, testing and technical documentation within agreed scope.

Issues that require vendor involvement can be escalated to the relevant vendor or partner under the agreed process.

Roles Organised Around Your SAP Landscape.

Depending on the service scope, the support organisation may involve the Head of SAP Department (overall coordination and, if needed, allocation of additional resources), the Head of Technical Support (support management and quality control), first-line SAP support specialists, module-specific technical support consultants, SAP developers (including ABAP), leading consultants and developers, SAP administrators and technical writers. A named team can be discussed when needed.

Ongoing SAP Support, with Options for Individual Requests.

Subscription SAP Support.

Ongoing SAP technical support for agreed systems, modules and operational tasks, with an initial service volume starting from two person-months. Service scope, coverage, SLA parameters and team arrangements are agreed individually.

On-Demand Support.

Support for individual SAP requests, with the initial service scope starting from one person-day.

Final service volume, coverage and commercial conditions are agreed for each contract.

Contract governance may also define customer-side approval roles, responsibility for changes to production systems and the documentation used for quality control, such as design documents, interface descriptions and user guides.

Service Parameters and Reporting Agreed by Contract.

Support coverage, incident priorities, response targets, escalation paths and service reports are established in the agreed SLA and support arrangement. Depending on the contract, reporting can include request status, service activity and agreed SLA/KPI indicators.
