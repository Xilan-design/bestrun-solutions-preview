Migrating from SAP ERP to SAP S/4HANA for Tcell

A large-scale ERP modernization project for one of the biggest telecom brands in Tajikistan

Client Information

Tcell is a mobile network brand in Tajikistan, owned and operated by the Tajik company Indigo Tajikistan, owned by AKFED (Aga Khan Fund for Economic Development). The Tcell network offers mobile telephony and mobile internet services in Tajikistan using GSM, UMTS, HSPA and LTE technologies.

Overview

SAP will end mainstream support for ECC ERP by 2027, prompting many companies to migrate to SAP S/4HANA. This transition offers significant modernization opportunities, as S/4HANA is built on the in-memory HANA database, supporting advanced technologies like AI. The shift reflects the need for more agile, scalable systems to meet modern business demands, since ECC’s aging framework limits its ability to adapt to current and future needs.

Tcell successfully migrated its ERP system from SAP ECC to SAP S/4HANA in partnership with Best-Run Solutions. The project lasted 6 months and included modernization key business processes, implementation of the newest SAP solution and corporate data migration.

Project results

Thanks to automation and AI, SAP S/4HANA streamlines many business processes. This improves efficiency, reduces redundancy, and provides a better customer experience. All of this can reduce business costs and boost revenue.
