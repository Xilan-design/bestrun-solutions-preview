Corporate intranet portal on WebSoft HCM for Ucell, one of Uzbekistan's leading mobile operators

Members of our team contributed to this project prior to joining Best-Run Solutions. The project was not fully delivered by our company.

About client

Ucell is a mobile network brand in Uzbekistan operated by COSCOM and one of the largest mobile operators in the country. The Ucell network offers mobile telephony and mobile internet services using GSM, UMTS, LTE and 5G technologies.

In 2019 Ucell was among the first in Uzbekistan to launch a commercial 5G network. Today the operator serves more than 11 million subscribers through over 25,000 base stations.

Overview

As Ucell prepared to scale its business, the company decided to create a single information space for all employees. Our team worked on a corporate intranet portal on the WebSoft HCM platform, which Ucell already used for employee learning, together with the E-Staff recruitment automation system.

The project covered the full cycle: installation, integration with the existing IT infrastructure, server cluster setup and in-depth customisation to Ucell's business processes. The goal was an intuitive, multifunctional portal that gives every employee the information and services they need in one place.

The team implemented about 600 functions. Key modules include a home page designed in line with the Ucell brand book, a news portal and events calendar, a personal account and employee profile, and workplaces for managers and timekeepers. Employees use the portal to access learning materials, choose corporate benefits, set goals and send thanks to colleagues.

The portal was adapted to the Uzbek market. Its design combines the Ucell brand book with national symbols of Uzbekistan, and the multilingual interface lets employees read news, learning materials and HR information in their preferred language.

Project duration: 6 months.
